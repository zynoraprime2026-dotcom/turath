About Turath

Turath (تراث, "heritage") is a database of Islamic manuscripts and historical events. It exists to preserve and share the intellectual heritage of the Ummah — the sciences, arts, history and scriptures that shaped civilisation for over fourteen centuries.

Built with a Vercel API and Postgres. Content is curated with respect; corrections are welcome via the admin panel.
